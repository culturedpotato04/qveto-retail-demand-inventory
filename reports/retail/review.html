Frozen model evaluation

Winner: random_forest_leaf_10, chosen on July-August validation MAE. Final test: 1 September-8 December 2011. Predictions are rolling one day ahead with actual previous-day observations. Substantial error remains; WAPE is not an accuracy score.

Inventory assumptions

  • Fixed supplier lead time: 3 days; extra cycle coverage: 7 days.
  • Normal approximation z = 1.645; achieved service level is unmeasured.
  • Independent, stationary daily demand and deterministic lead time.
  • Pack size: 12 units. Minimum order: 24 units.
  • Position = stock + on-order - backorders.
  • Continuous-review trigger at or below reorder point; order to the target, rounded up to packs.

Inputs live in config/inventory-demo.json. No inventory backtest or measured savings is available.
